# Measurement evidence and limits

The cost and tool examples in this skill summarize earlier computational runs.
Their raw provider receipts and output artifacts are not bundled with the skill.
Treat figures without a linked, inspectable record as historical observations,
not as prices or performance guarantees for a new target or provider account.

For a new campaign, record the selected model and revision, input hashes,
hardware, seeds, process exit status, output hashes, provider job IDs, and
billing rows in the run bundle. Join job IDs to billing rows before reporting a
settled campaign cost. Use current provider rates and a campaign ceiling for
planning; keep estimates distinct from settled charges.

The included [platform snapshots](evidence/claude-science-platform-skills.md)
record availability at the stated version and date. Discover current skills and
routes in the active session before choosing a tool.
